# Claude Opus 5 implementation review

Requested model: `opus`. The review was read-only and used the rewrite contract, implementation, notebooks and saved artefacts. Recommendations have not been applied automatically.

## Verdict

The feature clock, sparse admission, label construction and expanding-fit purge are causally sound, and the growth-IC statistics are reported with mostly appropriate hedging; the 87,081-row daily grid and the label/outcome accounting meet the contract. The allocation replay, however, cannot support any conclusion in either direction: it erases held positions when a vault dips under the TVL floor ($36k–$60k of equity vanishes per arm), breaks its own 33%/5% caps through post-clip renormalisation, force-sells at aged marks and sits in cash for the whole weekly regime, and its "incumbent" arm uses the indicator *defaults* (180/180/0.5/30-day gate/equal weight) rather than production's pinned parameters (360/45/0.6/14-day gate/inverse variance at 90 days).

## Calibration of reported conclusions

- **Growth IC (short-core Ridge).** Well calibrated for h ≤ 30: the 7-day "interval includes zero" and 14-day "marginal" statements are correct. For 45–90 days the point estimates are honest but the confidence is overstated — those intervals rest on 3–7 effective blocks, and the daily IC series contains at most ~3 non-overlapping outcomes per vault at 90 days. The monotone "IC rises with horizon" reading should be softened.
- **Short-core vs optional catalogue.** Differences (±0.01–0.06) sit inside the single-arm intervals and no paired uncertainty is reported; "improves 30-day, weakens 45–90" is over-read. The stated consequence ("does not justify selecting one universal catalogue") is fine.
- **Risk persistence.** "Remains strong" is not supportable from 4-date ICs; and the thin coverage is a bug (#4), not a data limitation as the summary implies.
- **Permutation null.** Scope is stated correctly and the limitation is not turned into a demand for old barriers. Wording tweak only (#12).
- **Allocation replay.** This is the mis-calibrated section. "Candidate arms lowered realised volatility and drawdown" and "a rejection of these two candidate allocator settings" attribute to the signal what is produced by erased holdings, cap breaches, forced liquidation and a non-production incumbent — the first 4.5 months are identical across arms. The correct statement is that the replay is not yet a valid test and neither rejects nor supports the candidates. "Not evidence for the 20–30% CAGR objective" is right, but for the wrong reason.
- **Coverage and counting.** 365 dates, 87,081 rows, separate daily-row / unique-outcome / matured-label counts, and the written-at-audit-only contract are all accurately stated.

## Choices to retain

- Fresh-mark-only feature clock at `date + 1 day 00:00`, `written_at` as provenance only, carried rows retained for audit but excluded from features.
- Sparse admission (two marks ≥ 24 h, 14-day recency, $7,500) with a per-date funnel; no age, density or long-window gates; ageing-out handled by the loop bound.
- Label design: first mark ≥ decision, first mark ≥ entry + h, ≤ 7-day delays, raw plus explicitly named scaled growth, `outcome_id` and duplicate weights summing to one.
- Expanding monthly refits with purge on actual `exit_ts ≤ boundary`, fold-local imputer/scaler, explicit warm-up statuses.
- `_daily_measures`: calendar-day closes, literal endpoints, 80% rule — the right template for fixing #4.
- Vectorised equal-date IC with the documented ranking convention; block bootstrap on the daily IC series; block-identity permutation as a scoped prediction-level null.
- Idealised close fill explicitly labelled; 2-day production cadence for all arms; adaptive vs fixed breadth on the same information.
- The weekly synthetic acceptance test and the separated daily-row / unique-outcome accounting.
