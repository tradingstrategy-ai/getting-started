## Scope and verdict

The first contribution completed 50 turns with read-only Read/Glob/Grep tools. Claude received the draft and retrospective audit and inspected relevant existing strategy/helper code. The second review completed one turn with tools disabled, reviewing the rewritten plan and the first contribution. It did not independently re-inspect code.

First verdict: the draft was too repetitive and its selected-entry calibration and uniform-exposure control were weak. Second verdict: **approve with bounded revisions**. We applied its concrete rule clarifications and separately corrected its missing-outcome bound interpretation. The resulting plan is an exploratory, conditional experiment, not a presumption that a profitable treatment exists.

## Corrections to the first contribution

We did not adopt every suggestion literally:

- Frozen-ledger negative-spell dollars are **headroom**, not an upper bound on a feedback-sensitive veto policy.
- Tagging initial units must allocate actual weighted-average-basis fee cash flows; a FIFO fee model would change economics.
- Capping a calibration slope at1 would prevent correcting a genuinely compressed forecast. The plan instead reports an uncapped nonnegative slope and its implied admission threshold.
- A random policy cannot use the informed policy’s future full-period veto frequency. Its rate comes from earlier causal C00 reference proposals only.
- A confidence interval containing zero is inconclusive, not an automatic demonstration that the feature cannot help.
- Old-accounting cells cannot be mixed with corrected-accounting cells to compute a current factorial interaction.

## Bounded second-review revisions applied

| Review point | Final rule |
| --- | --- |
| A negative pooled level or a one-name centre could create a level-only veto | Trading requires alpha>0, beta>0, at least two supported band signals and a supported non-pool D forecast; otherwise C00 pass-through. Report `−alpha/beta` and activation |
| Missing-outcome test had the wrong sign | Loss-to-zero is favourable to a veto. Mark-only carry is a scenario; positive unknown outcomes can erase gains. Show unknown vetoed dollars and break-even missing return, without a finite upper bound |
| Relative-D support and qualification target were ambiguous | Define the two-origin/two-supported-row/positive-variance identification rule separately from underlying D support; qualify against matured initial-unit managed spell outcomes |
| Fit and prediction centring had different missingness contracts | Fit z/y on the same matured signal-available pairs; predict on the current band; report partly matured origins and matched/global priors |
| Horizon alignment underweighted larger actual entries | Initial-dollar weights within entry dates, equal weights across dates; show winning/losing and censored cohorts |
| Veto state could grant phantom incumbency | Same state semantics as an unfunded minimum-ticket target, no refill or persistent slot reservation; trade parity until first veto |
| Actual source proposals can fall outside the derived band | Predict/report band ∪ proposals; out-of-band proposals pass through and their cohort discrepancy is investigated |
| Placebo shuffles change veto volume | Report totals and per resolved vetoed dollar; the prior qualification uses the per-dollar common-support comparison |

The second review called mark carry an adverse case. We narrow that wording: it is **not a mathematical adverse bound** when unknown positive outcomes have no finite upper bound. The plan reports the break-even missing mean instead. The final written amendments implement the bounded checklist; the second response predates those final amendments.

## Other ideas retained without adding trading arms

Claude proposed a risk tie-break at the rank 6/7 boundary and one-cycle entry confirmation. Both can be useful leads, but are not automatically appended as more experiments. Stage 0 counts informative versus address-only ties and first/repeated top-six entries. A boundary-swap or confirmation policy would need its own pre-registered comparison if those diagnostics show a plausible opportunity. Nearby substitution, hurdle and persistence studies are acknowledged in the audit.

## What the experiment can teach even if it stops

Capital-weighted lifetimes, initial-unit loss headroom, concentration of avoidable losses, re-entry delays, alignment of H2/H7 with managed spells, actual D coverage and within-band discrimination. A pooled-only or inactive forecast is a valid stop result; it is not a successful admission policy.

The plan retains limits on sparse economic quotes, hypothetical liquidation, assumed fees, unresolved reporting, conditional outcome support, exposure feedback and repeatedly researched calendars. Five engine placebos are a descriptive range, not formal statistical significance. Long replay is retrospective configuration evaluation.
